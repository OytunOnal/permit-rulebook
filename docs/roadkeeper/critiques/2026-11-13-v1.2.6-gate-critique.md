## Passes

**Label pass.** No option demeans its reader. "None of these yet — exploring my options", "I don't know" and "Not assessed yet" are there where they matter. Hesitations:

- "Doesn't apply to me, or I don't know" (salary, funds) merges two different answers.
- Salary bands to the cent: "€45,630 – under €45,934.20" makes a reader re-check a payslip for €304.
- "Assessed and not recognised" is accurate and hard, with no softer honest word.
- "Is your occupation on Germany's shortage list (IT, engineering, health…)?" then "Is your work in information or communications technology?" (3): the second reads as a trick.

**Prose pass.** Every string the walks produced was dumped to text (`*.txt` beside the screenshots) and read end to end.

- **Source-language residue:** the untranslated quotes (5); "Lebensunterhalt", "45% der Beitragsbemessungsgrenze", "Anlage zu § 20a", "Anmeldung", "talent salarié qualifié" as a threshold's label, "certificat de résidence", "jeune entreprise innovante"; route names glossed in parentheses (Chancenkarte, kennismigrant, zoekjaar, salarié qualifié).

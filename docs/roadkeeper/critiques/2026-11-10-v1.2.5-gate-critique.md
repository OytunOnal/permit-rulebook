## Passes

**Label pass.** No option demeans its reader. "None of these yet — exploring my options" and "I don't know" are offered where they matter. Hesitations:

- "Doesn't apply to me, or I don't know" (salary, funds) merges two different answers.
- "Not yet" for an age limit (7).
- Salary bands to the cent (19).
- "Vocational training, 2+ years (state-recognised where completed)" is clear.
- "Assessed and not recognised" is accurate and painful, with no softer honest word.

**Prose pass.** Every string the walks produced was dumped to text and read end to end.

- **Source-language residue:**
  - the untranslated quotes (5);
  - "Lebensunterhalt", "45% der Beitragsbemessungsgrenze", "Anlage zu § 20a", "Anmeldung", "the fiche names no window", "talent salarié qualifié" used as a threshold's label, "certificat de résidence", "umbral reducido";
  - route names glossed in parentheses (kennismigrant, zoekjaar, tarjeta azul-UE).
- **Modelling vocabulary:**
  - "quoted and dated · the checker asks about it, except 4 conditions" (country and route pages);
  - "Through the points path", "unlocking step";
  - "YOUR ANSWER — PICK ANOTHER TO CHANGE IT; ANSWERS YOUR NEW CHOICE STILL USES ARE KEPT";
  - "Set aside — after your change, no route uses these answers" reads well.
  - The result card's coverage line is plainer now: "checked against your answers, except 2 conditions we don't ask about".
- **Unexplained abbreviations:**

**Screenshot pass.** Every screenshot was opened beside `tokens.css`. No colour sits off the palette. The stamp red, met green, near amber, hold grey and band blue are used as the tokens describe, and the focus ring is the token's 2px stamp red. Rendering defects: the read date at value size (16), the § break (18), the stamp block on phones (17), the masthead offset (19). Seen but not a defect: a hover fill on the option under the last click (`d10-q9-desk.png`), and the verdict heading's focus ring after keyboard use (`h03-…`). The phone never scrolled sideways (`scrollWidth` 390) except in #320 (443).

## Recommended adjustments (prioritized, for the human's pick)

1. **Make the result say exactly what its cards say** (findings 1, 6, 7, 8, 9).
   - The verdict line counts every route short by the figure it names ("Two are short by up to €1,188/month").
   - The orientation year names its condition.
   - An age limit is listed as "not open at your age", not "not yet".
   - The French cards stop repeating quotes.
   - A met card leads with the quotes of the path it was met by.

   These are the sentences a reader takes away, and each one is checkable on the screen.
2. **Make the passport field keep its promise** (finding 2). Either add the countries' own names and common transliterations to the vocabulary, with accents folded as "México" already is, or change the hint and the no-match line to what is true ("English names; try the first letters").
3. **Ask only what can change this answer, then say where to start** (findings 3, 4; carried #189, #186).
   - Skip the job-search card's questions and the seven-year question once an offer route is met by a recognised degree.
   - When several routes are open, add one "Our reading" line on which one is the usual door.

The other findings become issues through `road`, labelled and folded as listed above.

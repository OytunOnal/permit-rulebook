## Prose pass (every user-facing string the walk produced, read away from the screen)

Collected from the hero, cards, rails, empty states and labels of all four walks. The three classic failures:

- **Source-language residue (by design, declared):** every quote is in German, French or Dutch and says so ("German, from arbeitsagentur.de"); route names carry the native term in brackets (kennismigrant, zoekjaar, salarié qualifié, tarjeta azul-UE, Chancenkarte). The English lines above each quote carry the meaning; the quote is the record. Accepted as the product's position (`/data/`: "Quotes are shown in the authority's own language and are never translated"). One residue outside that rule: "Fiche F16922" and "§ 18g Abs. 2 AufenthG" in the source lines, unexplained — tolerable in a citation.
- **Modelling vocabulary on user screens (F8):** "open gap, not a no"; "open unknown" ("6 NOT YET — 1 HAS AN OPEN UNKNOWN"); "Asked for: 4 routes in Germany"; "in our own words, not the authority's" as a stamp; "would come within reach"; "Needs where you studied"; "the route you came from"; "re-checked against the rules"; "each re-checked"; "The page also says:"; "Our reading, not the authority's words:" (dashed card, `c-desk-85`).
- **Unexplained abbreviations:** IND (explained once per card, then bare), MVV (explained inline, clumsily), BIG register (explained), ISCO-08 (explained inside a link), CEFR (in the question), BeschV / AufenthG / CESEDA / TFEU (in citations; TFEU and AufenthG are glossed once, BeschV once, CESEDA not), ICT (route names: "ICT Card — a transfer within your company (§ 19)" glosses it; "salarié détaché ICT" does not).

Other strings that read oddly aloud: "Gross monthly salary for the job you would do there (or monthly income you could show)?" — two questions; "Related skilled work experience in the last seven years?" — "related" to what, on a card that never asked the occupation; "Monthly money you could show for living costs (a blocked bank account, or a formal sponsorship undertaking)?"; "Permit Rulebook makes no immigration decision and no authority is bound by these results" — the one sentence everyone will read, and it is plain.

## Findings

One blocker, eleven friction, eleven polish. Screenshots in
`.run/critiques/2026-10-21-v1.2.1-gate-critique/`.

### Blocker

**B1 — On the lowest salary band the hero states the whole threshold as the
reader's shortfall.** *`55-hostile-results-short-by-full-threshold.jpg`,
`56-hostile-results-rail-open-floor.jpg`; `57-hostile-results-short-by-4274.jpg`
for the bounded band.* H declares "under €33,085.09". The hero: **"0 routes
look open. One is short by €45,630/year."** The card beneath it says "Up to
€45,630/year short" and the rail caption "up to €45,630 short" — the hero drops
the two words that make the sentence true. A reader earning €30,000 is told, in
the largest type on the site, that she is €45,630 short of a €45,630 line.
With the next band up the same hero prints the band's *maximum* shortfall as a
fact ("One is short by €4,274/year" for €41,356–€45,630). The product's one
promise is numbers a reader can trust; the first line of the result states a
number the site does not know. **Why a blocker and not friction:** it is the
hero, it is wrong by the reader's whole salary on the lowest band, and the
lowest band is the one a reader with a modest offer — the person the gap
analysis exists for — will pick. **A pass looks like:** the hero uses the
card's own words ("One is short by up to €4,274/year"), and on the open-floor
band names the line, not an amount ("One is under the €45,630/year line").

## Passes

**Label pass** (each option read in the persona's voice). Hesitations: "Not
assessed / not recognised" pairs Arjun's never-assessed degree with a refusal
(he picked "I don't know" instead, and the product treated it well); "No
completed qualification" is a fine label and H ticked it without shame;
"Doesn't apply to me, or I don't know" on living costs is two answers in one
row, and the product then treats it as the second ("an open gap"); "Over 40"
as the last age band with no "why we ask"; "Is your spouse or partner also
applying for (and eligible for) an Opportunity Card?" asks Deniz to know a
permit's name and his partner's eligibility in one tick. Nothing demeans.

**Prose pass** (every string of the walk read as a stranger, away from the
screen). Source-language residue: the verbatim quotes, by stated policy, with
the policy on `/data/` only ("Quotes are shown in the authority's own language
and are never translated"); route names carry the authority's term in brackets,
which reads as intended ("kennismigrant", "zoekjaar", "Chancenkarte", "salarié
qualifié"). Modelling vocabulary: "scored", "rule sets", "conditions this
interview did not ask", "in our own words, not the authority's", "scored against
your answers" on pages with no answers (P3). Unexplained abbreviations: BeschV,
AufenthG, ISCO-08, TVG, IND after one gloss, MVV, IELTS, Schengen, A1–C1 (P4).
Numbers: "short by €45,630" where "up to" was meant (B1); "€4,274" against
"€4,273.64" (P5); "13 of about 13" (F7). Grammar and case: "the Netherlands:"
at the head of a heading (P2); "The rules, quoted and dated:" as a deck ending
in a colon (reads as a label for the paragraph below, which it is not).

**Screenshot pass** (every screenshot opened as an image with `tokens.css`
beside it). Off-palette: nothing. Wrong case: P2. Style collision: the
unlock-step headings in the link blue (F4). Broken layout: none at 1920; at 390
the 404's button-before-sentence and the hanging "•" (P9). Rendering defect:
the rail's missing dot and missing band (F1) — the one token-level element
(`--rail-band`) that does not draw when it should. The 404 and `/data/` keep
the site tagline as their deck, as before.

## Recommended adjustments

Three, prioritised, in pick-and-apply form. **Apply all**, **apply 1 and 2**,
or **apply 1** — each stands alone.

**1 — Say only what the band says, and draw the band on every rail.** *(Closes
B1, F1, P5; lifts lenses 3, 5, 9.)* Rule: every sentence that names a shortfall
uses the band's own form — "short by up to €X" for a bounded band, and for the
open floor the line, not an amount ("under the €45,630/year line") — and the
hero uses the same words as the card. The rail draws the reader's band on
every card: a bounded band as the blue bar it already is; an open band as a
dot at the rail's edge with the caption the card already prints ("above the
amount"); never a tick on a blank track. The number is formatted once
(€4,274 or €4,273.64) and printed the same in both places.

not the link blue; the three counters either become in-page links to their

Gross annual salary in the offer

Threshold · 2026

The offer must pay at least €50,700/year. This is the general Blue Card threshold; a lower one exists for shortage occupations and recent graduates, on its own route.

A recognised university degree

Condition

Your degree must be one Germany recognises as equivalent to a German university degree. The checker asks where recognition stands — recognised, assessed as partial, not yet assessed — and if you do not know, it says so rather than guessing.

A job offer in Germany

Condition

The card is tied to an offer for qualified work. Looking for work without an offer is a different route — the Opportunity Card.

Also required — not checked here
  • An employment contract of at least six months
  • If your profession is regulated (health, law and similar), the professional licence must already be in hand

A non-EU passport

Who this is for

EU, EEA and Swiss citizens do not need a work permit in Germany, so this route does not apply to them. Citizens of Türkiye follow the same rules as other non-EU nationals to enter; the EU–Türkiye agreement adds rights once legally employed, not a different way in.

As an EU national you generally don't need a work permit to work anywhere in the EU. · europa.eu · read 2026-09-04 Official page ↗

## Findings

### Blockers

**B1 — A France reader with a research hosting agreement is offered the option,
then shown a rejection screen after two questions, with no word that the
French researcher permit exists.** *P3, `p3r-01-y0.png` (390) and the text
dump.* Q2 offers "I have (or expect) a hosting agreement with a research
institution there" for France; the interview ends on it and renders:

> "**Nothing open on these answers.** Your 2 answers were compared against 5
> published rule sets. … Change an answer to explore — every route below says
> what it asks for, and quotes it." · 0 OPEN · 0 WITHIN REACH · 5 NOT YET · "5
> routes need a job offer or an intra-corporate transfer"

`/france/` says "5 routes scored, 1 quoted" and lists no researcher route; the
exclusion list lives on GitHub. The passeport-talent *chercheur* is a route this
person can actually hold — the one-pager's own v1.1 criterion for a quoted page.
**User cost:** the affected reader is told, on a site whose claim is "every route,
quoted and dated", that nothing is open, when the truth is "your route is not
covered here". He leaves believing the first. This is the record's known failure
shape (a rejection screen for a person the rules do not reject) on a new
persona. *An answer that ends the interview is the strongest form of "an answer
that changes nothing": Q2's research option for France leads nowhere and should
say so before it is picked.*

**B2 — On a phone, tapping a "You declared" row to change an answer leaves the
question off-screen; the reader sees the footer or the unchanged panel, and
after re-answering is returned to the bottom of the new result, not its
headline.** *P1, `p1c-01-edit-viewport.png`, `edm-01-edit-landing.png`,
measured at 390.* After `Yearly salary ✎`: `scrollY 1811`, question card top at
**−1,366 px**, viewport showing "Permit Rulebook makes no immigration decision…"
and the footer columns. After `German ✎`: `scrollY 924`, card top **−479 px**,
viewport showing the "You declared" list, identical to before the tap. Focus
stays on `<body>` in both. After picking a new band the page rendered the new
verdict ("1 route looks open. One is short by €4,274/year.") and landed at
`scrollY 8088` — the panel again; the headline is ~8,000 px up. Desktop does
not have this: the panel is a sidebar and the card top lands at −15 px.
**User cost:** the correction control — the one thing the panel exists for —
looks dead on a phone. A reader taps, nothing visibly happens, and either taps
again or gives up; the one who scrolls up and finds the question, answers it,
and is dropped at the bottom never sees what changed.

### Friction

**F1 — The result addresses an offer-holder as a job-seeker, and ranks steps by
rule count rather than by what the reader can do.** *P1, `p1r-05-y3376.png`;
P2, `p2r-01-y0.png`; H.* Under "Steps that would unlock more": "Your job-search
permit above is exactly for this — lawful time in the country to land that
offer." — to a reader who declared "I have (or am about to get) a job offer
there". The three step groups are ordered *intra-corporate transfer · research
hosting agreement · full German recognition* for a person whose only real step
is the third; the four-country hero says "Nearest: an intra-corporate transfer
in Germany" to someone with no employer, and the unknown-heavy hero "Nearest: an
intra-corporate transfer" to an offer-holder. **Cost:** the differentiating
section opens with two steps the reader cannot take and a sentence that
contradicts their declaration.

**F2 — The experience ladder is not a ladder, and the engine treats it as one.**
*Q7, `p1-08-after-unknown.png`; results for `y2in5` vs `y3in7` compared.* The
options are `Under 2 years` · `2+ years within the last 5` · `3+ years within
the last 7` · `5+ years within the last 7`. Two are true at once for most
readers; "3+ within 7" does not imply "2+ within 5", yet `y3in7` scores
Experienced worker (§ 6 BeschV, "two years of recent skilled work in the last
five") as CRITERIA MET exactly as `y2in5` does. **Cost:** a reader with three
years of work six-to-seven years ago is told a route is met that its own quoted
rule closes. *(Third run for the overlapping bands — previous F19.)*

**F3 — Every route page warns "the last run did not reach one of them" as if one
of its own sources were unread; `/data/` says the unread source is German.**
*`route-02-y844.png`; `/france/employee-card/` text.* The sentence "a daily
check re-reads every source; the last run did not reach one of them" appears
verbatim on the German route page **and** on the French employee-card page. On
`/data/`: "A German source did not answer on the last run; the values it backs
were read on 2026-09-07." **Cost:** on 27 of 28 route pages the liveness line
casts doubt on sources that were read fine, and never names the one that was
not.

**F4 — The not-yet Opportunity Card hides the points tally, the one number that
decides it.** *P2, text dump; compare P1's open card "6 points — 6 needed ·
English +1 · Experience +2 · Shortage list +1 · Age +2".* Priya's card prints
her two unknowns, the Anabin and ISCO links and six quotes, but not "5 points —
6 needed". The unlock list then says "would be met" six times (full
recognition, partial recognition, 5+ years, German A2, German B1, German B2 or
higher) — three of them rungs of one ladder. **Cost:** the reader nearest to a
verdict cannot see how near. *(The salary-ladder enumeration of previous B3 is
gone; this is its smaller cousin on the CEFR enum.)*

**F5 — A returning visitor lands mid-interview with no notice.** *`resume`
walk; `p-resume` text: "QUESTION 3 OF UP TO 8 / Your highest completed
qualification?".* Answers are restored from the device and the reader is put
on the next unanswered question; the only clue is the collapsed "You declared —
2 answers" box below the card on a phone. The pre-scoped entries do this well
("France is on the record now. Your answers about … are kept; France asks 2
more questions") — the plain `/` entry says nothing. **Cost:** a second visit
reads as a broken first question. *(Previous F18, still open.)*

**F6 — After each answer, keyboard focus drops to `<body>`.** *ctrl walk:
"after Enter/click on opt: focused: BODY".* The focus ring is present and
visible (2 px `--color-stamp`), the counter is a polite live region — but the
next question's options are not focused, so a keyboard reader Tabs through the
header (wordmark, Menu / Countries, Check yours, The data, Feedback) before
every question. **Cost:** five extra keystrokes per question on desktop, a
lost place on every one.

**F7 — In the four-country result, France and the Netherlands are collapsed to
one line, and the line hides what it counts.** *P2, `p2r` DOM: `DETAILS.country-sec
:: France 5 not yet` (closed), `Netherlands 6 not yet — 1 has an open unknown`
(closed).* The Netherlands' open unknown — the orientation-year designation she
answered "I don't know" to — is the most reachable thing on her screen, and it
is behind a closed summary with no name. **Cost:** the country most likely to
open for a fresh graduate under 30 is the one folded away.

**F8 — The Spanish researcher card says a hosting agreement is "not checked
here" to a reader who just declared one.** *P3′, text dump: "Also required —
not checked here: A hosting agreement — the formal agreement a research
institution signs to take you on — or an employment contract with the research

device ("not checked here") is spent on something that was asked, which teaches

**F9 — Two hints ask for knowledge the reader does not have.** *Q5
(`p1-06-after-pick_T_rkiye.png`), Q8 (`p1-09-after-y3in7.png`).* "Not sure?
§ 18g AufenthG … lists the shortage groups by ISCO-08 code (132, 133, 134, 21,

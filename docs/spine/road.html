How a slice closes

A scenario in the human's language is approved before any code. A builder makes it green in a worktree and never touches the ledgers. Two reviewers read the diff — one against the standards, one against the scenario — and the review is recorded with the commit it read. The session walks the preview; the human walks it. Then one word, merge: the merge is the deploy, the live read is the proof, and the slice is real-green. Fixes ride under the open version; the stamp comes when the last candidate lands.
